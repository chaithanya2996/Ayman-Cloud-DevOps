On-Premises vs Cloud:
--------------------- 
On-Premises means the company owns and manages everything in its own data center. 

Ex:
A bank maintaining its own servers in its own building. 

Responsibilities:
The company Manages:
. Physical Servers
. Networking
. Storage
. OS
. Security
. Backup
. Updates
. Application

Cloud:
The cloud provider manages physical infra, and the customer uses services through the internet

Ex: 
Creating a VM in Azure instead of buying a physical server

Comparison 
On-Premises			Cloud
===========================================
Company buys hardware		Company rents resources
High Initial Cost		Low Initial Cost
Takes days/weeks to set up	Takes Minutes
Company manages data center	Cloud provider manages data center
Scaling is difficult.		Scaling is easy
Hardware failure is		Cloud provider handles
company responsibility		hardware level issues.
